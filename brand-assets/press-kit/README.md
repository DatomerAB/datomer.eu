Generated: 2026-10-29

## About

Pär is a local personal AI application built by Datomer AB in Stockholm, Sweden.
Ask questions, explore ideas and draft replies with AI on your device, without
a central Pär server storing your conversations or personal context.
Financial-assessment and confidential contract-review workflows are in development,
not dependable features of the current beta. You review the results and decide
what happens next. Optional online services may send information to their providers.
Features depend on your plan, device and setup.
