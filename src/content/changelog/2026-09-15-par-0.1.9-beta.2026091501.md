# Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026091501

## What’s new in Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026091501

### Changed

- **Home Hub Tab in Profile**: Relocated Pär Home Hub management from Settings to a dedicated tab inside Profile (`desktop/src/components/HomeHubSettings.tsx`), introducing a clean pill-tab navigation pattern (General, Personas, Security, Home Hub) with deep-linking support (`/profile?tab=homehub`).
- **Streamlined Settings**: Removed Home Hub section and device/member pairing logic from Settings, focusing Settings strictly on system, server, vault, and data maintenance.
