# Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026091601

## What’s new in Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026091601

### Fixed

- **Persistent Companion Auth & Cookie Fallback**: Added dual-layer token persistence via both `localStorage` and a persistent 1-year `par_hub_token` cookie with automatic header decoration across all HTTP and SSE streaming calls (`/api/chat/stream`, `/api/chat/file`, routines, and knowledge sync). This eliminates unexpected re-prompting for PINs after initial pairing on mobile Safari and Chrome.
- **Companion App Lock Bypass**: Disabled host-level desktop inactivity auto-lock on companion mobile browsers, ensuring paired phones and tablets stay continuously connected without being blocked by host desktop PIN prompts.
- **Companion Onboarding Auto-Bypass**: Ensured paired companion devices automatically bypass desktop first-launch wizards and jump directly into the conversation view.
- **Brand Logo & Mobile Polish**: Replaced placeholder icons with the official rounded Pär brand logo on the welcome and pairing screens, and added responsive layout scrolling with pinned action buttons.
