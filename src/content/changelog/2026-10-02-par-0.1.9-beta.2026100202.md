# Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026100202

## What’s new in Pär v0.1.9-beta.2026100202

### Added

- **Consistent theme-aware Pär branding** across sidebar/mobile headers, chat
  welcome and assistant avatars, onboarding and pairing. Native, tray, PWA
  installation and browser icons derive from the same supplied artwork, with
  reproducible asset generation and contrast checks across all ten themes.
- **Chat response display preferences** in Settings -> Chat: completed progress
  defaults to collapsed and may be hidden; response details are separately
  collapsed by default and may be hidden. Preferences are local, available on
  every tier, and cannot disable answer cleanup.

### Fixed

- **Internal protocol text leaking into chat answers.** A shared output parser
  suppresses recognized reasoning/system/metadata blocks before streaming,
  including split markers and supported native role/channel formats, while
  preserving literal code. Chat, attachments, combined actions, summaries and
  meeting notes reuse the same final-answer policy.
- **Displayed answers differing from saved answers.** Streams now deliver the
  authoritative final answer; the frontend replaces provisional text and
  rejects stale updates. Copy/export, memory, persistence and routine reports
  consume clean finalized content rather than superseded stream attempts.
- **Legacy reasoning accordions exposing internal text.** Restored assistant
  answers are normalized; suspicious unverified cached bodies are masked until
  restored or regenerated. User messages and original database rows are not
  bulk-rewritten.

### Changed

- Chat stream starts and non-streaming responses advertise output-policy
  version 1. The frontend withholds unverified deltas and rejects older backend
  answers rather than displaying potentially raw internal text. Update the
  backend and frontend together, rebuilding the Python sidecar for installed
  releases. No live-model inference was used for the regression/browser checks.
